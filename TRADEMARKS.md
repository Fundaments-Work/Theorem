# Trademark Policy

The code of Theorem is open source under the [MIT License](./LICENSE). You can use, copy, change and redistribute it, including commercially.

The **name and logo are not covered by that license.** "Theorem™", the Theorem logo (`public/favicon.svg` and the icons generated from it) and "Fundaments" are trademarks of Fundaments. This policy explains how you may use them. Its purpose is simple: people should always be able to tell whether they are getting Theorem from us or a version made by someone else.

## You may, without asking

- **Talk about Theorem.** Write reviews, tutorials, articles, videos and comparisons that name Theorem and show screenshots.
- **Say your work is compatible.** "Works with Theorem", "A template for Theorem" or "Export for Theorem" are fine, as long as you do not suggest we made or endorse it.
- **Redistribute unmodified builds.** Package an official release for a Linux distribution, a package manager or an app store under the Theorem name, as long as you do not change the code apart from what packaging requires (paths, build flags, dependency versions).
- **Build from source for yourself or your organisation.** Personal and internal builds may keep the name.
- **Use the logo to link to us,** unchanged, next to a mention of Theorem.

## You need to rename if you

- **Distribute a modified version (a fork).** Choose a different name and logo that will not be confused with Theorem. A note such as "based on Theorem" in your description or About screen is welcome.
- **Offer a paid or hosted service** built on Theorem's code, such as a sync server, a book store or a hosted reader. Do not call it Theorem or use our logo.
- **Use the name in a product, company or domain name,** such as "Theorem Pro", "TheoremBooks" or `theorem-reader.example`.

Forks must also change the app identifier (`work.fundamentals.theorem`) and the update endpoints, so neither project's users receive the other's updates.

## Always

- Do not change the logo, or combine it with your own marks.
- Do not suggest that we sponsor, endorse or support your product or service when we do not.
- Do not use the name or logo in a way that is misleading, or that harms the project's reputation.

## Asking for permission

If you are not sure whether your use is allowed, or you want to use the name in a way not covered here, open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/fundaments-work/theorem/discussions). We are glad to say yes to things that help readers.

This policy may change as the project grows. The version in the `main` branch is the current one.
