# Theorem Features

Everything Theorem can do, grouped by what you are trying to do. Each line links to the guide page that explains it step by step. For installation and first steps, start with the [user guide](index.md).

Platform notes are given where a feature is limited. Features marked "needs the desktop or Android app" are not in the browser demo.

## Your library

- **Many formats.** EPUB, Kindle (MOBI, AZW, AZW3), FictionBook, comics (CBZ, CBR) and PDF. [Add books](library-and-import.md#add-books)
- **Import whole folders.** (new in 1.6.0, not released yet) Scan a folder, keep its folder structure, and browse your library by source folder. Needs the desktop or Android app. [Import a folder](library-and-import.md#import-a-folder)
- **Search, sort and filter.** Find books by title, author and more, and narrow the view by status. [Search, sort and filter](library-and-import.md#search-sort-and-filter)
- **Edit book details.** Change titles, authors, covers and tags; open a book in its own window on desktop. [Open and manage a book](library-and-import.md#open-and-manage-a-book)
- **Act on many books at once.** (new in 1.6.0, not released yet) Select all results across a filtered library, then mark read, add to a shelf or delete. [Select many books](library-and-import.md#select-many-books)
- **Daily highlight.** A highlight from your own notes resurfaces on the Library page. [Daily highlight](library-and-import.md#daily-highlight)

## Organizing

- **Shelves.** Group books your way, with sub-shelves for a second level (sub-shelves are new in 1.6.0, not released yet). [Create a shelf](shelves.md#create-a-shelf), [Sub-shelves](shelves.md#nest-shelves-with-sub-shelves)
- **Smart shelves.** (new in 1.6.0, not released yet) Shelves that fill themselves from rules such as reading status, tags, series, authors and formats. Needs the desktop or Android app. [Build a smart shelf](shelves.md#build-a-smart-shelf)
- **Series.** Volume order, combined progress, and a "Read next" prompt when you finish a volume. [Organize books into series](shelves.md#organize-books-into-series)

## Finding books

- **Discover.** Hand-picked free books with search over about 78,000 titles, newest editions, shelves and subjects, plus your school, university or library catalog or your own OPDS server (Calibre, Komga, Kavita and others) as tabs, with sign-in for private catalogs. Works on desktop and Android. [Discover](discover.md)

## Reading

- **Reader.** Themes (light, sepia, dark), type and layout controls, in-book search, table of contents and page navigation. [Change how a book looks](reading.md#change-how-a-book-looks), [Search inside a book](reading.md#search-inside-a-book)
- **Footnotes and citations** open in a small popover without leaving the page. [Footnotes and citations](reading.md#footnotes-and-citations)
- **PDFs.** Zoom modes, page labels, and Present and Print on desktop. [Read a PDF](reading.md#read-a-pdf)
- **Comics.** Page by page, or a continuous vertical strip for webtoons (the strip is new in 1.6.0, not released yet). [Read a comic](reading.md#read-a-comic)
- **Speed Read.** Shows one word at a time at a speed you choose. Off by default, and not available for PDFs. [Speed Read](reading.md#speed-read)
- **Separate window.** Open a book in its own window. Desktop only. [Open a book in a separate window](reading.md#open-a-book-in-a-separate-window)

## Highlights, notes and words

- **Highlights in six colors**, merged automatically when a selection overlaps or touches an existing one in the same chapter (not for PDFs). [Highlight text](highlights-and-notes.md#highlight-text)
- **Notes and bookmarks.** [Add a note](highlights-and-notes.md#add-a-note), [Bookmark a page](highlights-and-notes.md#bookmark-a-page)
- **PDF markup.** Draw and annotate directly on PDF pages. [Mark up a PDF](highlights-and-notes.md#mark-up-a-pdf)
- **Share a highlight as an image.** [Share a highlight as an image](highlights-and-notes.md#share-a-highlight-as-an-image)
- **Workbench.** Every highlight, note, saved word and bookmark across your library in one place, with filter, sort and search. [Workbench](workbench.md)
- **Dictionary and vocabulary.** Offline dictionaries (MDict and StarDict) with an online fallback, and a vocabulary list you can review and export. [Look up a word](dictionary.md#look-up-a-word), [Install an offline dictionary](dictionary.md#install-an-offline-dictionary), [Review and export your vocabulary](dictionary.md#review-and-export-your-vocabulary)

## Listening

- **Read aloud (Immersion Reading).** Your system voice reads the page and turns it for you. [Read a book aloud](listening.md#read-a-book-aloud)
- **Neural voice.** A natural-sounding voice that runs offline after a one-time download. Desktop; on Android through a companion speech engine. [Install the neural voice](listening.md#install-the-neural-voice-desktop), [Neural voice on Android](listening.md#neural-voice-on-android)
- **Audiobooks.** Attach your own `.m4b`, `.m4a` or `.mp3` with chapters, speed control and a sleep timer. [Attach an audiobook](listening.md#attach-an-audiobook), [Play an audiobook](listening.md#play-an-audiobook)
- **Generate an audiobook** from a book with the neural voice. Desktop only. [Generate an audiobook](listening.md#generate-an-audiobook-desktop)

## Feeds

- **RSS and Atom feeds** with an in-app article reader, offline saving and retention control. [Feeds and articles](feeds.md)

## Your data

- **Markdown export.** Keep highlights and vocabulary as plain files for Obsidian or Logseq, with built-in note styles and your own template. Desktop app only. [Markdown export](vault-sync.md), [Write a custom template](vault-sync.md#write-a-custom-template)
- **Sync between devices.** Pair devices directly with a QR code, with no cloud account. Needs the desktop or Android app. [Sync between devices](device-sync.md)
- **Backups.** Create a backup file, and see or clear your storage. [Data and storage](settings-and-shortcuts.md#data--storage)
- **Everything stays on your device.** There is no account and no cloud service.

## Progress and habits

- **Statistics.** Reading time, streaks, a heatmap and achievements. [Reading statistics and goals](statistics.md)
- **Goals and reminders.** Daily and yearly goals, with optional notifications. [Set your goals](statistics.md#set-your-goals)
- **Share your stats** as an image. [Share your stats](statistics.md#share-your-stats)

## Settings and control

- **Settings and keyboard shortcuts.** Every setting by tab, and the shortcuts for the whole app and the reader. [Settings and shortcuts](settings-and-shortcuts.md), [Keyboard shortcuts](settings-and-shortcuts.md#keyboard-shortcuts)
- **Command line.** A terminal interface on Linux desktop. [Use the command line](settings-and-shortcuts.md#use-the-command-line)
- **Updates.** The desktop app can check for updates itself; on Android, install a newer APK by hand. [Install Theorem](install.md)

If something does not work as described, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
