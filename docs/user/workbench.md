# Workbench and Bookmarks

Review every highlight, note, saved word and bookmark across your whole library, search them, edit notes, and jump back to the place in the book.

![The Workbench page listing color-coded highlights from a book, with All, Highlights, Notes and Words tabs](../screenshots/workbench.png)

## Open the Workbench

Click Workbench in the sidebar (the second icon), or press `Ctrl+4`. On Android, tap Workbench in the bottom bar. The page header counts your annotations and saved words, for example "4 annotations • 3 terms".

## Filter, sort and search

1. Use the tabs to choose what to see: All, Highlights, Notes, Words or Bookmarks. Words are the terms you saved from the dictionary. Bookmarks opens the Bookmarks page, described below.
2. Click Cards to switch from the list to a card view, and click List to switch back. The button is hidden on the Words tab.
3. Use the sort menu to choose Newest First, Oldest First or By Book.
4. Type in the search box at the top to search highlights, notes and books.

Each entry shows its type, date, the book and author, and the highlighted text. Highlights keep the color you chose, shown as a colored edge. Long quotes and notes are shortened, and a Show more link expands them.

## Work with an entry

Click the three-dot menu on an entry to:

- **Open in book** (or Open in article for RSS articles) opens the reader at that highlight.
- **Edit note** opens a box where you change the note text.
- **Share** opens the share menu. See [Highlights and notes](highlights-and-notes.md#share-a-highlight-as-an-image).
- **Delete** asks you to confirm and then removes the highlight and its note.

If you opened the Workbench from a book, a bar reading Showing annotations for shows that book. Click Clear to see everything again.

## Review saved words

1. Open the Words tab.
2. Click a word to see its phonetic spelling and all saved definitions.
3. Click Delete this term in that window to remove it.

To save words in the first place, see [Dictionary and vocabulary](dictionary.md).

## Bookmarks

Bookmarks are the last tab in the Workbench, so you can reach them on every screen, phones included. You can also press `Ctrl+7` to jump straight to it. The Bookmarks page shows the same tabs at the top, so you can move between your highlights, notes, words and bookmarks without going back to the sidebar. Choosing Notes there, for example, takes you to the Workbench on the Notes tab. On a narrow screen the tab you are on shows its name and the others show an icon, so all five always fit.

1. Use the first menu at the top to show bookmarks from All sources or from one book or article. Each book is listed with its bookmark count.
2. Use the second menu to sort by Newest First, Oldest First or By Book.
3. Type in the search box to search bookmarks, books or authors.
4. Click a bookmark, or choose Go to bookmark (Open article for RSS articles) from its menu, to open the reader at that spot.
5. To remove a bookmark, choose Delete from its menu and confirm.

Each bookmark shows its position, such as a PDF page number or the percentage through an article. To create bookmarks, see [Highlights and notes](highlights-and-notes.md#bookmark-a-page).
