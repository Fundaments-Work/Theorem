# Reading Statistics and Goals

See your reading time, streaks and a 12-week heatmap, set daily and yearly reading goals, turn on reminders, and share a statistics card as an image.

## Open the Statistics page

Click the bar chart icon at the bottom of the sidebar, just above Settings, or press `Ctrl+6`. On Android the icon is in the top bar.

The page shows:

| Section | What it shows |
| :--- | :--- |
| Total Books | How many books are in your library, with how many are completed and in progress |
| Reading Time | Your total time spent reading |
| Today's Reading | Minutes read today against your daily goal |
| Streak | Your current streak in days and your best streak |
| This Week | Reading time over the last seven days |
| Reading Activity | A heatmap of the last 12 weeks, from Less to More |
| Reading Goals | Progress bars for today's minutes and for books finished this year |
| Recently Read | Books you opened lately |
| Annotations | Counts of highlights, notes and bookmarks, and highlights by color |
| Favorites | Your favorite books |
| Achievements | Badges you have earned |

While you have a streak, a flame icon with the number of days also appears at the bottom of the sidebar.

## What counts as reading time

Theorem counts time while a book is open and visible. It pauses when the window is hidden or the app goes to the background, and starts again when you return. Days, streaks and goals use your local time zone.

A book counts as completed when you reach about the end of it, or when you mark it read yourself. A streak is a run of days in a row with some reading. The heatmap covers the most recent 12 weeks.

## Set your goals

1. Open Settings and select the General tab.
2. Under Reading Goals, set Daily Reading Goal, from 1 to 180 minutes per day. The default is 30.
3. Set Yearly Book Goal, from 1 to 100 books per year. The default is 24.

The Statistics page then shows progress against both numbers.

### Notifications and reminders

In the same Reading Goals section:

- **Goal Notifications** (on by default) tells you when you meet or fall short of your daily goal.
- **Daily Reminder Time** (20:00 by default) sets the time of a reminder if you are behind your goal for the day.
- **Sync Notifications** (on by default) tells you when a device sync finishes or fails.

These use your operating system's notifications. On Android, Theorem may ask for notification permission.

On Android the daily reminder is kept by the system, so it arrives even when Theorem is closed and after the phone restarts. It is skipped on a day you have already met your goal. Under the reminder time, Settings shows when the next one is due. Two things can stop it, and Settings shows a note with a button for each instead:

- **Notifications are off for Theorem.** Tap **Allow notifications**.
- **Android may deliver the reminder up to an hour late.** From Android 12, an app needs the "Alarms & reminders" permission to remind you at the exact minute, and Android 14 and later don't give it by default. Tap **Remind me on time** and turn it on.

Some phones (Xiaomi, Huawei, Oppo, Vivo and others) also stop apps in the background to save battery. If reminders still don't arrive, set Theorem's battery use to "No restrictions" in the phone's settings.

On a computer the reminder shows while Theorem is open.

| First Book | Complete your first book |

| On Fire | Read 7 days in a row |

## Share your stats

1. On the Statistics page, click Share Stats.
2. In the Share Reading Stats window, choose Square (1:1) or Story (9:16), and a look: App Theme, Dark, Tinted or Sepia.
3. Click Download to save a PNG. On Android the image goes to your gallery. Where it is offered, click Share... to use your device's share sheet.

## Statistics and sync

Reading statistics are included in [device sync](device-sync.md). When two devices have different totals, Theorem keeps the larger value, combines the daily activity from both, and recalculates your streaks.

## Weekly rhythm, XP, and levels

Choose a weekly reading rhythm in Statistics or Settings → Reading Goals. Four
days is the suggested starting point; you can choose one through seven or turn
the weekly goal off. Five minutes accumulated across books and sessions qualifies
a reading day. Library shows your progress for the current Monday–Sunday week,
with **View reading progress** opening Statistics. Goal edits begin next Monday.
Your daily-minute goal and daily streak are separate.

A qualifying day earns 10 XP. Meeting a weekly goal earns another 20 XP. Every
100 lifetime XP advances a level, starting at level 1. Missing a week never takes
XP away. Extra minutes and app opens do not earn extra XP, and no reading feature
is locked behind a level. Awards begin when you choose a rhythm; older reading
stays in your existing statistics.

To keep Library quiet, turn on **Hide motivational progress in Library** in
Statistics or Reading Goals. Statistics still shows your progress. XP and level changes appear inline in Statistics without opening a celebration
over the reader.

Reading-rhythm data syncs between upgraded devices. Older app versions keep
working with their existing statistics but cannot record these new counters or
reliably pass the extension between upgraded devices. Pair upgraded readers
directly when using a mixed-version group. Backups include your XP; a full restore
returns it to the backup's state, and syncing unions awards peers still hold.
