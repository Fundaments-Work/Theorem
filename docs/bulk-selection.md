# Bulk selection

Choose **Select Books** in the library toolbar. The selection bar appears even before any books are selected.

- **Select all results** selects every book in the current filtered result, including rows outside the visible scroll area. It respects search, shelf, source folder, reading status, favorites, and unshelved filters.
- **Clear selection** keeps selection mode active; **Done** exits it.
- Click a book, or focus it and press Space or Enter, to toggle selection. Grid, list, and compact views all show selection state.
- Changing filters clears the selection. Sorting keeps it. Books that disappear from the results are removed from the selection; newly added books are not selected automatically.
- Bulk actions remain disabled until at least one book is selected and search results have finished updating.

The existing bulk read/unread, shelf, export, and delete actions use this filtered selection. Delete confirmation is limited to the books selected when the dialog opened and still selected when confirmed.
