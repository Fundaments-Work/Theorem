# Sub-shelves

Create a shelf from Shelves and choose an optional **Parent shelf**, or open a top-level shelf and choose **New sub-shelf**. Open the parent to browse its sub-shelves. The back button on a sub-shelf returns to its parent.

Edit a shelf to change its parent or choose **None (top-level shelf)** to promote it. Shelves with children must remain top-level; promote or move their children before moving the parent. One level of nesting is supported.

Each shelf keeps its own book membership. A parent does not automatically include its children's books. Shelf search includes both levels. Deleting a parent preserves its children as top-level shelves and never deletes library books.

Parent relationships are persisted and included in peer sync. Older libraries remain top-level. Missing parents, cycles, and unsupported deeper nesting are normalized to top-level shelves so they remain accessible.
